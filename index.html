Powertrain, 2026–27 car, in development

The 90° box

A four-wheel-drive car has to send power from the gearbox out to a driveshaft running the length of the car. This compact right-angle box makes that turn, and I'm leading its design.

Notes

  1. Pair of steel bevel gears, 1:1 ratio, turns the gearbox output 90° into the driveshaft.
  2. Module 4 to match our gearbox. Gears bought off the shelf so the team can build on schedule with the tools we have.
  3. Split casing, designed for the smallest possible package. Left half fits a 139 × 140 × 49 mm envelope.

ME 374C, five-person team, spring 2026

Predator 79cc engine

Intake redesign, proven on a dyno we built

To show a new intake worked, we first had to build the instrument to measure it: a belt-and-drum brake dynamometer with a machined aluminum drum and a Hall effect speed sensor. I wrote interrupt-driven Arduino firmware that streamed engine speed to MATLAB. The new intake used a 3D-printed manifold with a swept 45° bend and a lathe-turned 6061 aluminum velocity stack.
